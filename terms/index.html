Free trial and plans

  • DashLink starts with a free trial with every feature unlocked. What it includes is shown in the app and may change.

  • A purchase made on your phone with DashLink Wireless is switched on within 24 hours. Where we switch a plan on for you directly (for example where Google Play isn’t available), we confirm the payment details with you before you pay, and switch it on within 24 hours of payment.
  • A one-time purchase lasts for as long as we offer and support DashLink on that head unit.

Using DashLink safely

You are responsible for driving safely and obeying traffic laws. Only interact with the screen when it is safe and legal to do so. Set up DashLink, themes and Racing sessions while parked.

Speed warnings, speed readings and reminders are aids only. Always rely on your car’s own instruments, road signs and your own judgement.

DashLink Racing: closed courses only

Use DashLink Racing only on closed courses: track days, rallies on closed roads, recce, and private test venues where you have permission. Never use lap timing, performance tests, the road dyno or any other Racing feature on public roads.

Motorsport is dangerous. You take part at your own risk and are responsible for following the venue’s and organiser’s rules. Check your event’s rules on electronic devices before using DashLink at a rally, regularity (TSD) or other event.

Pace notes are generated automatically from your recce and can be wrong. Always check and edit them before relying on them. The co-driver voice, coach calls and timing are aids, not a replacement for your own judgement or an official timing system.

On first use, DashLink Racing asks you to accept its safety terms. Those terms form part of these terms.

Figures are estimates

Fuel used, fuel left, range, trip cost and dyno power are estimates based on GPS, the distance you drive and the details you enter. They are not measurements from your car’s sensors and may be inaccurate. Lap and test times depend on GPS and sensor quality. Don’t rely on them for anything where accuracy matters, such as official results, tax or legal records.

Bluetooth music control depends on your head unit’s Bluetooth system, and phone sensors need DashLink Wireless on a compatible phone. Use the free trial to check before you buy.
