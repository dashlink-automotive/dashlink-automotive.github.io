Home screen

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

One screen to connect, see your drive and reach your apps. Big targets you can hit without looking twice.

  • USB, Wireless or Self mode

    Pick how you connect, then tap one big button. Pair once over Bluetooth and Wireless Android Auto reconnects on its own.

  • A live drive card

    Speed, compass heading and GPS status, with this trip's distance, driving time and average speed.

  • Your apps, pinned

    Maps, music and calls a tap away. Suggestions to start with, and you can pin your own any time.

  • Quick settings

    Dark theme, auto-start, performance mode and Dock mode, plus a live check on your phone, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.

03
Car care

Your car
remembers for you.

DashLink counts your kilometres as you drive and tells you what's due next, by distance or by date.

227,506km

  • Service reminders

    Oil change, tyre rotation or anything you add. Each one shows if it's on track.

  • Done in one tap

    Mark a service done and the next one is scheduled automatically.

  • A maintenance log

    Every service you've done, with its date.

The next service also appears on your home screen.

04
Fuel & costs

Know your range.
Know your cost.

Tell DashLink about your car once. It estimates fuel left, range and what each trip costs from the distance you drive.

L/100 kmkm/Lmpg (US)mpg (UK)

  • "I filled up"

    One tap after a full tank. You can also log a top-up or set the level yourself.

  • Trip cost in your currency

    Enter your fuel price and see what each drive costs. It shows in the Journey layout too.

Range, fuel left and costs are estimates based on the average consumption you enter. Always check your instrument cluster for real fuel data.

Fuel left and range feed straight into Dock mode.
