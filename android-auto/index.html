USB Android Auto

Plug the phone in. That’s it.

DashLink switches the phone into the right mode by itself and connects. Unplug and plug back in, and it recovers cleanly.

  • No settings to find on the phone
  • Charges while you drive
  • The most stable link on any unit
Wireless Android Auto

Leave the phone in your pocket.

The phone connects over the head unit’s own Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, the same way factory wireless systems do. Works with current Android Auto versions.

  • No adapter or dongle to buy
  • Needs a head unit with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth
  • DashLink Wireless helps phones start the connection
Self mode

No phone needed.

For head units with Android Auto installed, self mode runs it from the head unit itself.
