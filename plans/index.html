How to buy

Two ways to pay.

  1. 01

    On the head unit

    Buy through Google Play right in DashLink: open Go Premium, if your unit has Google Play.

  2. 02

    Or on your phone

    Scan the QR code on the head unit and pay on your phone with DashLink Wireless. A phone purchase is switched on within 24 hours.

  3. 03

    Then tap Restore

    With the head unit connected to the internet, tap Restore. After that your plan works offline. Step by step.
