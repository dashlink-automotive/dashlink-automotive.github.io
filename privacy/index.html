1. Who we are

DashLink is an application that enables Android Auto on Android-powered car head units. This website and the DashLink service are operated under the name DashLink, based in Pakistan, by an individual trader rather than a registered company. You can reach us using the details in Contact us.

This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. For the purposes of data protection law, we are the data controller for that information.

If you have any question about this policy, contact us at dashlink.support@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

We only collect information you give us. There is no tracking pixel, advertising tag or third-party analytics script on this site. The DashLink app on your head unit is covered by its own separate policy.

When you request a licence

The form on our purchase page asks for the following.

Full nameYesTo address you and identify your request
WhatsApp numberYesTo arrange payment and confirm your licence

Collected automatically with your request

  • Your approximate country — derived from your network connection or your browser's timezone, used only to show prices in your local currency and recorded with your request so we quote you consistently.
  • The currency and price you were shown, and the date and time of your request.

We do not store your IP address with your request, and we do not build a profile of you or make any automated decision that produces a legal or similarly significant effect.

3. Why we collect it

  • To answer your request — checking compatibility, setting up your 7-day free trial, and issuing a licence if you decide to buy.
  • To support you — your head unit details let us help you far faster when something doesn't work.
  • To keep proper records — of what was quoted, to whom, and when.
  • To improve compatibility — knowing which panels and chipsets people actually own tells us what to support next. We use this in aggregate.

If you are in the UK or the EU: our lawful basis is performance of a contract or steps taken at your request before entering one (Article 6(1)(b) UK/EU GDPR) for handling your trial request, and legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) for keeping records and improving device compatibility.

We do not use your details for marketing unless you separately ask us to.

4. WhatsApp

Trials, licence keys and support are delivered over WhatsApp, because that is how most of our customers prefer to be reached. By giving us your WhatsApp number and submitting the form, you are asking us to contact you there.

Important: WhatsApp is operated by Meta, not by us. When you message us, your data is also handled under WhatsApp's own privacy policy, which we do not control. If you would rather not use WhatsApp, email us instead at dashlink.support@gmail.com and we will handle your trial entirely over email.

5. Where it's stored

When you submit the form, your request is saved in this website's own database, on our web hosting, and a copy is emailed to our team so we can act on it. Both live on servers operated by our hosting and email providers on our behalf.

We take reasonable measures to protect it: the site is served over HTTPS, administrative access requires an account, and your details are never published on the public website or exposed through any public interface.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach ever affects your personal data and poses a risk to you, we will tell you and the relevant regulator as the law requires.

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, and we never have. We share it only with the service providers needed to run the service:

  • Our web hosting provider — stores the website and its database.
  • Our email provider — delivers the notification of your request and our replies to you.
  • Our network and security provider — routes traffic to the site and protects it from attack.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — if you choose to communicate with us there.

We may also disclose information where we are legally required to, or to establish or defend legal claims.

7. How long we keep it

We keep trial requests for 24 months from your last contact with us, so we can support you, honour your licence and keep accurate records. After that we delete them.

You can ask us to delete your details sooner — see Your rights. Where we are required to keep certain records for tax or accounting purposes, we will keep only the minimum required, for the required period.

8. Cookies and browser storage

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies, and there is no Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or similar on these pages.

WordPress sets a cookie only if you log in as an administrator of this site. That does not apply to ordinary visitors.

The purchase page does use one item of your browser's local storage — data kept on your own device, which is never transmitted to us:

KeyPurpose
dashlink_order_v1Remembers what you typed into the licence request form so refreshing the page doesn't lose your progress.

You can clear it at any time by clearing site data in your browser. Nothing on this site stops working if you do.

9. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Access — get a copy of what we hold about you.
  • Correct — fix anything that is wrong or out of date.
  • Delete — erase your details from our records.
  • Restrict or object — limit how we use your information.
  • Port — receive your details in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent — including asking us to stop contacting you on WhatsApp.

Email dashlink.support@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. We will not charge you, and we will not treat you differently for asking.

If you are in the UK or EU and you think we have handled your data badly, you may complain to your national data protection authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

10. International transfers

We operate from Pakistan and serve customers worldwide, and our hosting and email providers may process data in other countries. This means your information may be transferred outside the country where you live, including outside the UK and the European Economic Area.

Where we transfer personal data out of the UK or EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses with our providers.

11. Children

DashLink is a product for car owners and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us their details, contact us and we will delete them.

12. The DashLink app

This policy covers this website — browsing it and submitting a trial request.

The DashLink application you install on your head unit has its own privacy policy, which explains what the app collects on your device and why. That policy is the authoritative one for the app; nothing on this page changes it.

One thing worth knowing, because it spans both: your licence is issued to your head unit, so the device your licence is registered to can be linked to the contact details you gave us on this website. We keep that link only to issue, validate and support your licence — for example to confirm a licence is yours when you contact us, or to move it to a replacement unit. If you ask us to delete your data, that link is deleted along with your trial request.

Android Auto itself is Google's own software. When you connect your phone, your navigation, media and messaging are handled by Google and by the apps you use, under Google's privacy policy — not by us. DashLink is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Google LLC.

We may update this policy as the service changes. The "last updated" date at the top always reflects the current version. If we ever make a change that materially affects how we use your personal information, we will tell affected customers directly rather than relying on you to re-read this page.
