Compatibility

Will it work on my head unit?

  • Android 6.0 or newer on the head unitSettings → About on the unit shows the Android version.
  • USB host support, for USB modeMost units have it. The phone plugs into the unit’s USB port.
  • Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, for wireless modeThe phone connects over the unit’s own Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.
  • Android Auto on your phoneFor USB and wireless modes. Self mode needs Android Auto installed on the head unit instead.
  • An Android 7.0+ phone for DashLink WirelessOptional. Needed for sending sessions, the phone library and phone sensors.

Not sure? Try DashLink free and see, or message us your unit’s details.

Troubleshooting

Quick fixes.

Use a good data cable (not a charge-only one), plug into the head unit’s main USB port, unlock the phone and accept any prompts. DashLink switches the phone into the right mode itself, so unplug and plug back in once.

Make sure Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are on, on the head unit. Open DashLink Wireless on the phone to help start the connection. If it still won’t connect, try USB to rule out the phone.

DashLink recovers from a black screen automatically. On slower units, turn on Performance mode in settings.

Resolution and orientation are applied automatically. If something looks off, tell us your unit’s screen size and we’ll help.

Music info comes from your phone through the head unit’s Bluetooth system. Some units don’t pass it on.
