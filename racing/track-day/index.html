Track day lap timerPro

Every lap, timed to the line.

Laps are timed by crossing the start/finish line, interpolated between GPS fixes rather than snapped to the nearest point. The line lays itself, or set it with one tap.

  • Flying or standing starts: stop on the grid and the clock starts as you pull away
  • Automatic sector lines after lap 1, with sector times
  • Live delta to your best lap
  • Upcoming pace notes on top, with the distance to the next one
Ghost carPro

Race your best lap.

The yellow arrow on the chase map is your best lap, driving with you. When it pulls ahead you’re losing time; when you catch it, you’re on a personal best. Pace notes ride along on the same map.

  • Your best lap, replayed in real time
  • See exactly where it pulls away
  • Pace notes marked on the road ahead

Phone sensorsProNew

Your phone becomes a precision timing sensor.

A typical head unit reads its GPS about once a second. At 120 km/h that’s one reading every 33 metres, and a lot happens in a corner in 33 metres. Mount a phone running DashLink Wireless and its GPS and motion sensors fill in the gaps.

  • 25/s
    Far more detailUp to 25 readings a second instead of about one. Start lines, sectors and speed traces are pinned down far more precisely.
  • g
    Real g-forcesBraking, cornering and acceleration measured by the phone’s motion sensors, not estimated from GPS.
  • Nothing to loseIf the phone drops out, DashLink carries on with the car’s GPS by itself. The data goes only to your car.

Set up in a minute

Mount it. Pair it. Drive.

  1. 1
    Mount the phoneIn a holder on the dash or windscreen. Not in a hand, cup holder or pocket.
  2. 2
    Start Racing on the head unitTap the phone icon at the top and choose Auto. In DashLink Wireless, tap Start, then your car.
  3. 3
    Let it calibrateStopped, keep still for 2 seconds. Then on a straight, pull away firmly for 2 seconds above 20 km/h.
  4. 4
    DriveTurn the phone’s screen off if you like. The sensors keep running until the car closes Racing or you tap Stop.

Phone sensors are part of DashLink Pro on the head unit. DashLink Wireless is free, for Android 7.0+ phones.
