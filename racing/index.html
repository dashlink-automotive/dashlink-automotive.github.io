Performance
Track day lap timerPro

Every lap, timed to the line.

Laps are timed by crossing the start/finish line, interpolated between GPS fixes rather than snapped to the nearest point. The line lays itself, or set it with one tap.

  • Flying or standing starts: stop on the grid and the clock starts as you pull away
  • Automatic sector lines after lap 1, with sector times
  • Live delta to your best lap
  • Upcoming pace notes on top, with the distance to the next one
Ghost carPro

Race your best lap.

The yellow arrow on the chase map is your best lap, driving with you. When it pulls ahead you’re losing time; when you catch it, you’re on a personal best. Pace notes ride along on the same map.

  • Your best lap, replayed in real time
  • See exactly where it pulls away
  • Pace notes marked on the road ahead

Rally stage timerPro

Start line to stop control.

Standing start at the start line, or a flying start. Finish at the line or at the stop control. Known stages are recognised when you arrive, with the pace notes ready.

For rallies on closed roads. Check your event’s rules on electronic devices.

Recce → pace notesPro

Drive it slowly once. Get pace notes.

On the recce, DashLink writes the pace notes: corner, severity, length, tightens and opens, crests and cautions. Add your own calls and voice memos. On the stage, a co-driver voice calls them. A pace-note editor is included.

Check your event’s rules on electronic devices.

Performance testsFree

0–100, with every split.

Launch, braking and overtake tests, timed from the moment you move. Splits at 50, 60, 80, 100 and 120 km/h with the distance each took, plus 60 ft, ⅛ and ¼ mile with trap speed. Every run is saved and compared with your best.

  • 0–100 launch, 100–0 braking, 80–120 overtake
  • The launch moment is detected precisely
  • “Saved, ready for the next run”: no buttons between runs
Road dynoFree

A power curve from one pull.

Do a pull on a closed, level road and get your power at the wheels, with gear changes detected, the share lost to air drag and rolling, and your best run laid over the top. Power-to-weight puts the car in a class, and your garage keeps each car’s weight and body type.

An estimate of power at the wheels, from GPS speed and your car’s weight, on a level road with no wind. Engine power is usually 10 to 20 percent higher. A 10 Hz GPS gives a finer curve.

Live modesPro

Sport. Track. Race.

Three live screens, one tap apart. Upcoming pace notes stay on top in every mode, with the distance to the next one, and the cabin tints as you gain or lose time.

Track: lap time, live delta to your best and sector times, beside a heading-up chase map. Stop at the line or roll across it, and the clock starts by itself.

Session analysisPro

Where the time goes, in plain words.

Best lap, theoretical best and consistency. The corners that cost you, and why: braking early, slow apex, late on the throttle. Lap and sector tables, speed traces against your best, gap to best, a map coloured by speed or gain/loss, a full-screen zoomable map, a grip (g-g) plot with cornering, braking and acceleration figures, and a corner-by-corner tab.

Shown here in DashLink Wireless, with a session sent from the car.

Big-screen debrief Pro New

Take the session to the pit wall.

Open every session on a laptop or a big screen with DashLink Wireless: corner losses, lap and sector tables, speed traces and the grip plot, big enough for the whole team.

  • No cloud or account: your phone serves it
  • Over Wi-Fi or the phone’s hotspot
  • Made for driver and coach debriefs

CoachPro

Sector calls while you drive, and a spoken debrief after the session.

Track library

A map with coloured sectors, direction arrows and numbered corners. Length, corners left and right, the longest straight, your best lap, the pace notes and every session you’ve driven there. Tap Drive to start. Import and view free; drive a session with Pro.

Import any track

A closed loop becomes a circuit (a START / FINISH waypoint sets the line). Anything else becomes a stage, with pace notes generated for you. Import and view free; drive a session with Pro.

Phone sensorsProNew

Mount your phone and it becomes a timing sensor for the car: up to 25 readings a second instead of about one, and real g-forces.
