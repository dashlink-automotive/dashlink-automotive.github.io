Will it work for me?

If your dashboard screen runs Android, DashLink runs on it.

There's no brand list and no approved-device list. DashLink is built for the ordinary Android screens people actually buy — whatever the shop called it when you had it fitted.

Your head unit

Most Android-powered car screens running Android 6.0 and above. No root required on the majority of units.

Your phone (optional)

Any Android phone on Android 8.0+ for USB and Wireless modes. Not needed at all in Self Mode.
