Fuel & costs

Range and cost, estimated honestly.

Range, fuel left and each trip’s cost, all estimated from the distance you drive. Tap “I filled up” to keep the estimate on track.

  • km/L, mpg (US) or mpg (UK)
  • Your own fuel price and currency
  • No sensor or adapter needed

Oil change, service, tyre rotation, brake check, insurance, registration, or your own. Due by distance or by months, with “due soon” and “overdue” status and a maintenance log with date and odometer.
