Safety

A warning at the speed you choose.

Set the speed you don’t want to go over. DashLink keeps an eye on it so you can keep yours on the road.

  • A red badge when you go overThe dial panel turns red and shows how far over you are. It’s also shown on top of Android Auto.
  • A gentle chimeA short double beep, at most every 30 seconds, so it reminds without nagging.
